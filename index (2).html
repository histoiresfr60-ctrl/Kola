Our Story

A Moroccan sanctuary
unlike any other

Nestled on the outskirts of Marrakech, Riad Qodwa is a boutique haven surrounded by over 1,000 species of exotic plants. Facing the breathtaking Atlas Mountains, we offer an authentic Moroccan experience — 20 minutes from the vibrant medina, yet worlds away from the hustle.
